2. What data is processed

2.1 Account data

When you create an account: your email address, a password (stored in encrypted form and never accessible to the publisher), and the display name you choose. If you use Google sign-in, your email address, name and Google identifier are passed to the Application by Google.

2.2 Game data

The information generated as you play: your best times per circuit, difficulty, direction and lap count, penalties recorded during a run, and the display name shown on leaderboards and in the live activity banner. When you challenge someone to a duel, the circuit, settings and a shareable link are created; each participant enters their own display name when they play their attempt — no data about a third party is entered on their behalf.

2.3 Technical data and analytics

Usage data is collected through Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): pages and screens viewed, general usage events (races started or completed, duels created), device type, operating system, language, and approximate location derived from the IP address.

Note for the publisher: unlike SwingUp, F1 Finger Race does not currently show a consent banner before Analytics starts collecting data. To match what this policy describes and to comply with French/EU rules on non-essential trackers, a consent banner should be added before publishing this policy live — happy to build one if useful.

6. Your rights

Under the conditions set out in the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Access (Art. 15): obtain a copy of your data.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): correct inaccurate data. Your display name and email address can be changed directly in the Application, under “My account”.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): request deletion of your account and data.
  • Restriction (Art. 18) and objection (Art. 21).
  • Portability (Art. 20): receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)) at any time for analytics, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Post-mortem directives: decide what happens to your data after your death (Art. 85 of the French Data Protection Act).

These rights can be exercised at developpement24.web@gmail.com. A reply will be sent within one month, extendable by two months for complex requests. Proof of identity may be requested in case of reasonable doubt.

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you may lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07 — www.cnil.fr, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

7. Trackers and analytics

The Application uses your browser's local storage in order to function: keeping your session and remembering your language and settings between visits. These are strictly necessary for the service you request and do not require your consent.

Analytics through Firebase Analytics does place trackers subject to consent. See the note in section 2.3 above about the consent banner still to be added. Declining does not limit the Application in any way. To change your choice once a banner is in place, clear the site data in your browser settings.

8. Security

Exchanges between the Application and its servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, encrypted at rest. The Firebase configuration values embedded in the Application's code are public client identifiers, not secrets; access to your data is actually restricted by Firestore security rules, which limit reading and writing of account, time and duel records to the account holder (and, for a duel, its participants).

As no system is infallible, the publisher undertakes to notify any data breach to the CNIL within 72 hours, and to the individuals concerned where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

9. Minors

The Application is not intended for people under 15 years old. In France, a minor's consent to the processing of their data in an online service is valid only from that age; below it, the authorisation of the holder of parental responsibility is required. No data is knowingly collected from a child under 15 without such authorisation.
